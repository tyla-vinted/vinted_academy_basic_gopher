## NewAvatar
Return an Avatar built with a struct literal: Avatar{...}
Name the fields: Avatar{Name: name, Pos: ...}
Pos is a struct too: Pos: Pos{X: x, Y: y}

## DistanceTo
The distance is the length of the line from p to q: the square root of dx*dx + dy*dy.
math.Hypot(dx, dy) computes it for you. Add import "math".
return math.Hypot(q.X-p.X, q.Y-p.Y)

## CanHighFive
Use your DistanceTo on the two positions: a.Pos and other.Pos.
Compare the distance with 100.0. "At most" means <=.
